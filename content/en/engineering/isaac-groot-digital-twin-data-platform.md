This project connects robot data from simulated and physical environments to NVIDIA Isaac GR00T N1.7 data conversion, model training, policy inference, and physical-robot execution. My core responsibility was robot control and end-to-end system integration.

## Scope and Ownership

- Completed the end-to-end path from data conversion and model training to inference and physical-robot testing.
- Owned robot control, policy-service integration, and the physical inference path.
- Integrated robot control and workflow automation with the recording system through ROS 2 and WebSocket.

## End-to-End Data and Inference Flow

1. Organize robot data from simulated and physical environments for training and inference.
2. Convert robot data into the GR00T N1.7 training format, then train and evaluate policies through inference.
3. Connect the policy service to robot control and validate the physical-robot path.

## Engineering Focus

- Defined clear interfaces between data, model services, and robot-control commands across integrated systems.
- Separated policy inference from robot output behind inspectable service boundaries and validated the physical-robot path.
