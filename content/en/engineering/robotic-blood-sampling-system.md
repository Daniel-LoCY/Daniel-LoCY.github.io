This was a student competition project completed for the National Smart Manufacturing Application Competition, separate from my master's thesis research. Working with a team, I integrated depth sensing, computer vision, and collaborative robotic-arm control into a prototype workflow supporting blood-draw tasks.

## Highlights

- Used Azure Kinect and OpenCV for vessel localization.
- Used YOLOv8 for tool and task-state recognition.
- Integrated perception, task execution, and robotic-arm control into a single prototype workflow.
